Gift cards

Every gift card bought, one row per card. Source: Arbitrage Card's gift card export. Paid = face value minus the export's discount; Cost = paid minus Arbitrage Card cash back (cash-back cards show 0% discount). AC balance is what Arbitrage Card's export says is left; Used (orders) is what the store's order emails show the card paid (matched by store + last 4 digits; Kohl's so far) and Est. left is face minus that — "shared" means several of your cards end in the same 4 digits, so the total is split between them. Balance is one you checked yourself (0 marks it used). Card numbers and PINs stay hidden until you click them.
