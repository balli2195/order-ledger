The rule

  1. Days 0–13 below the 12% ROI price: Watch. Most drops are short; selling into them costs ~10 ROI points per unit.
  2. Day 14+: 14-day average Buy Box ROI ≥ 12% → Hold. It was a bounce or a short dip.
  3. Average < 12% and flagged → Liquidate. Flag = cheap competitor stock would take 30+ days to clear.
  4. Stranded (from Amazon's stranded inventory report): shown with the reason, Amazon's suggested fix and the auto-removal date, whatever the price rule says. Fix the listing or create a removal order before the removal date.
  5. Average < 12%, not flagged → Recheck next week. About 71% recover within 30 days.
  6. Would be Liquidate, but you've already lowered your Seller Snap min below the 12% price and it's at or under the Buy Box → Liquidating. Nothing to change. If the min is lowered but still above the Buy Box → Min above Buy Box.
  7. Liquidate now means your Seller Snap min is still at a normal level. Each one gets a suggested min 5% under the Buy Box (or under the lowest FBA offer when there's no Buy Box), added to your approvals sheet. Tick the ones you want and ask Claude to apply them.
  8. Would be Liquidate, but your last-7-day sales clear your stock within 30 days → Selling through. You're already selling it off at your current repricer min, so there's nothing to change.
ROI = (Buy Box × (1 − referral %) − FBA fee − IL cost − $1 prep) ÷ IL cost
Days to clear = cheap units ÷ (Keepa monthly sold × rank boost ÷ 30) · flag at ≥ 30 · no Keepa estimate: flag at ≥ 25 cheap units
Cheap units = stock of live FBA + FBM offers priced below your 12% ROI price · rank boost = median BSR 30d ÷ 7d, only if > 1, max 3×

Selling through items show how many days your stock will last at the last-7-day pace. If one is still selling at 12%+ ROI, keep an eye on it: in the backtest, flagged items that sold well early on mostly stalled afterward.

Why this rule (backtest, Apr–Oct 2026)

~550 price drops across 400 ASINs you sold, IL costs, Keepa Buy Box and per-seller stock (only sellers live on each day). Decision at day 14, drops with 14-day average ROI under 12%.
