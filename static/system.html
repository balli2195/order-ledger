How it all fits together

Everything that runs your Amazon business: the scheduled tasks Claude runs for you, the jobs the Hub runs on its own, the Hub's pages, and the outside services they talk to. This page keeps itself up to date. New Hub pages and Hub jobs show up the moment they're added to the code; a check every night at 3:31 AM finds new or changed scheduled tasks and writes their explanations here. You never need to ask for something to be added.
